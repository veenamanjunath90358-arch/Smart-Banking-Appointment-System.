About / Help

This is a frontend-only Smart Banking Appointment System demo. Everything runs in your browser and data is stored in localStorage. Use the tabs above to switch between the User Portal and the Bank Portal. The app simulates real-time updates by re-rendering both views when actions occur.

How it works

  • Log in as a Customer and create appointment requests.
  • Log in as Bank Staff to view pending requests (filter by bank or status).
  • Accept or Reject requests and add a short note — updates will show in the Customer view instantly.

Privacy Note

All data is saved locally to your browser (no server). If you clear your browser data or open in another browser, the stored data won't be available. For multi-tab syncing, the app listens to storage events (so actions in other tabs/windows also update).
